Sambal for iPhone

What is Sambal?

Sambal is an iPhone app that saves recipes from any website without the clutter. Its cook mode shows the ingredients, the steps and the timers on one screen, so you can follow a recipe while your hands are busy.

How do I import a recipe?

Share a link to the recipe page with Sambal, or paste the link into the app. Sambal fetches the page and saves the recipe without the ads, pop-ups and life stories.

How do timers work, and why do notifications matter?

Cook mode lets you start timers for the steps that need them. When your phone is locked or Sambal is in the background, a notification is what tells you a timer has finished. Please allow notifications for Sambal in iOS Settings, otherwise you will not hear the timer while the phone is locked.

How do I move my recipes to a new phone?

On your old phone, open Settings in Sambal and export a backup. Move the backup file to your new phone, then open Sambal there and import it from Settings.

Good to know: backups contain your recipes, but not the photos you added yourself. Those have to be added again on the new phone.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Sambal, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple account you bought Sambal Pro with.

What is free, and what is Sambal Pro?

The free plan lets you save up to 15 recipes. Importing, cook mode and timers are free. Sambal Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the limit, so you can save unlimited recipes.
