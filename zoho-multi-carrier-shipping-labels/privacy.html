Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 · Untapped, a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) of Camiel Landheer, Amsterdam, KvK 93478221

1. Introduction

Untapped, a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) of Camiel Landheer, Amsterdam, KvK 93478221 ("we", "our", or "us"), a Dutch software development studio, is committed to protecting the privacy of organizations ("Licensees") and end-customers whose data is processed through our software. This Privacy Policy outlines how data is accessed, transmitted, and safeguarded when you install and operate the Multi-Carrier Shipping & Labels extension within Zoho CRM.

Our extension is designed in strict compliance with the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / AVG) and adheres to Zoho Marketplace security guidelines.

Core Privacy Principle: Our extension acts purely as a stateless API bridge. We do not store, harvest, analyze, or monetize any CRM customer records or personal data on our own external database servers.

2. Data Accessed Within Zoho CRM

To provide its intended functionality—specifically, calculating shipping rates, generating carrier shipping labels, processing returns, and fetching Track & Trace statuses—the extension accesses the following specific data fields from your Zoho CRM workspace:

  • Contact & Customer Details: Contact Name, Account/Company Name.
  • Address Information: Street address, house number, postal code, city, state/province, and country code (from Sales Orders, Contacts, or Accounts modules).
  • Communication Fields: Customer email address (used strictly for carrier return label generation and automated tracking emails) and phone number (required by specific carriers like DHL and DPD for delivery SMS notifications).
  • Shipment Metadata: Sales Order ID, order reference numbers, parcel weight, and physical dimensions.

3. Data Transmission & Third-Party Carriers

When an authenticated user clicks "Create Shipping Label" or initiates a return in Zoho CRM, the extension retrieves the necessary order details and transmits them directly via secure, encrypted HTTPS REST APIs to the shipping carrier selected by the user (such as PostNL, DHL, SendCloud, or MyParcel).

This transmission occurs exclusively between your Zoho CRM environment and the official API endpoints of the chosen carrier using your organization's authorized API credentials. We do not intercept, reroute, or mirror this traffic through proprietary storage servers.

Once transmitted, the processing of personal data by the shipping carrier is governed by that carrier's own respective privacy policy and Data Processing Agreement (DPA) with your organization.

4. Data Retention & Storage

Because the Multi-Carrier Shipping & Labels extension functions as a pass-through middleware application:

  • No External Retention: Untapped does not retain or store customer personal data, shipping addresses, or order histories on external servers.
  • Local CRM Storage: All generated shipping labels (PDF files), return labels, tracking numbers, and shipment records are stored directly and securely within your own Zoho CRM cloud infrastructure as attachments and custom module records.
  • Carrier Configuration: Your carrier API keys and public secrets entered during installation are stored encrypted inside Zoho CRM's native custom module storage.

5. Security Measures

We enforce rigorous security standards to protect all data flows handled by the extension:

  • All API communications between Zoho CRM and third-party shipping networks are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 or higher).
  • The extension executes inside Zoho's secure embedded iframe sandbox, preventing cross-site scripting (XSS) and unauthorized data leakage.
  • We conduct regular static code reviews and dependency updates to ensure ongoing compliance with Zoho Marketplace security requirements.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

Under the GDPR, data subjects (your customers) hold rights regarding their personal data, including the right to access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), and restriction of processing. Because all personal data processed by our extension is stored within your own Zoho CRM database or transmitted to your contracted carriers, any data subject requests should be fulfilled directly through your Zoho CRM admin console or carrier dashboards.
