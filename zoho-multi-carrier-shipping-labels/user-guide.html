User Guide

Daily workflow manual for sales representatives and logistics operators

1. Overview & Core Features

The Multi-Carrier Shipping & Labels extension seamlessly integrates your logistics workflow directly inside Zoho CRM. Without ever leaving your CRM interface or logging into separate carrier web portals, your sales and fulfillment teams can:

  • Generate and print instant shipping labels for PostNL, DHL, SendCloud, and MyParcel.
  • Automatically sync real-time Track & Trace links back to the Zoho CRM Sales Order.
  • Let customers pick local service points via an interactive map (Service Point Picker).
  • Generate and email automated return shipping labels in seconds.

2. Generating a Shipping Label (Standard Flow)

To create a shipping label for a confirmed sales order, follow these simple steps:

1
Open a Sales Order Navigate to the Sales Orders module in your Zoho CRM workspace and click on the specific record you wish to fulfill. Ensure that the Shipping Address fields (Street, Postal Code, City) are populated.
2
Launch the Shipping Widget In the top-right button bar of the Sales Order detail screen, click the custom button labeled 🚚 Shipping & Returns. This will open the embedded fulfillment modal.
3
Select Carrier & Package Type In the modal, click your desired carrier logo badge (PostNL, DHL, SendCloud, or MyParcel). Next, select the specific package service from the dropdown menu (for example: Standard Parcel, Mailbox Package, or Global/World Parcel).
4
Verify Dimensions & Create Label Review the package weight (pre-filled from your order items) and adjust the dimensions (Length x Width x Height in CM) if necessary. Click 🚀 Create Shipping Label.

✅ Instant Fulfillment: Within seconds, the carrier API processes the request. The generated PDF shipping label is automatically attached to your Zoho CRM order under the "Attachments" related list, and the Track & Trace link is activated!

3. Using the Service Point Picker (Pickup Locations)

If your customer prefers to pick up their parcel at a local convenience store or postal drop-off point instead of receiving home delivery, you can use our built-in location selector:

  1. Inside the shipping modal, look at the 🚚 Delivery Method toggle and switch from 🏠 Home Delivery to 🏪 Pickup Point.
  2. Click the blue button labeled 📍 Select Pickup Point.
  3. An interactive map or location list will open, automatically centered on the customer's postal code.
  4. Select the nearest pickup location (e.g., PostNL Pakketpunt - 350m). The store name and street address will be locked into the shipment summary.
  5. Proceed to generate the label as normal. The carrier will route the parcel directly to the chosen service point!

Note on Mailbox Parcels: When selecting PostNL or MyParcel Mailbox packages, ensure the thickness does not exceed 3.2 cm. Our widget includes built-in validation that will warn you if dimensions exceed carrier limits, preventing costly shipping surcharges.

4. Tracking Active Shipments in CRM

Once one or more labels have been generated for an order, clicking the 🚚 Shipping & Returns button will open the Active Shipments Dashboard instead of the label creation form.

From this overview screen, you can:

  • View Live Status: See whether the package is Announced, In Transit, or Delivered.
  • 1-Click Tracking: Click directly on the clickable tracking number to open the carrier's live tracking portal in a new tab.
  • Create Extra Labels: Click ➕ Extra Label if the order requires multiple boxes or a split shipment.
  • Cancel / Void Labels: Click the trash icon (🚨 Cancel This Label) to void a shipping label with the carrier before it is handed over to the driver.

5. Generating Return Shipping Labels

Our extension makes customer returns effortless by generating pre-paid or standard return labels directly from the original sales order:

  1. Open the Active Shipments dashboard on the order and click the button ↩ Return.
  2. The widget will automatically scan the linked CRM Contact or Account to pre-fill the customer's email address.
  3. Select a Return Reason from the dropdown (e.g., Customer Request, Wrong Item, or Damaged).
  4. Click ↩ Generate Return Label.

The carrier generates a dedicated Return ID and return PDF label. Depending on your carrier settings, this label can be automatically downloaded or sent to the customer so they can drop off the return package.

6. Need Help?

If you encounter unexpected carrier errors or need help configuring your permissions, please consult our FAQ & Troubleshooting Guide or reach out to your system administrator.
